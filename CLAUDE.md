## Good Neighbors Wildlife standing rules (from the owner)

These rules apply only to Good Neighbors Wildlife (the site at the repo root: `app/`, `components/`, `lib/`; live at www.goodneighborswildlife.ca). They do not apply to Wasp Problem (`wasp-problem/`), which has its own rules.

- Business: Good Neighbors Wildlife, humane wildlife removal in Toronto, York Region, Durham Region and Peel Region (raccoons, squirrels, skunks, birds, bats), plus sealing the entry point and repairing the damage the animal caused. Written for owners of larger, higher-end homes: calm, careful and respectful of the home, with nothing meant to scare.
- Going live: the live site deploys from `claude/good-neighbors-v2-visual-redesign` (Vercel). The site code on the repository's default branch is older than the live site, so start Good Neighbors work from the live branch. Work on the session's own branch, show the owner every new page and all new wording first, and merge into the live branch only after the owner approves. The branch `claude/docs-snapshot` is a read-only snapshot of ads-chat docs for the far-towns chat (ads chat, Oct 11); never merge it into the live branch. The far-towns chat (a Claude Code chat) works on its own branch, `claude/far-towns-plan`.
- SEO on every change, without being asked: each page title and H1 names the animal or problem and Toronto (a service-area page names its own area instead). Titles end in " | Good Neighbors Wildlife" through the layout template, so pass `pageMetadata` a bare title. Give each page its own meta description of about 150–160 characters, link pages to each other with descriptive anchor text, keep structured data accurate (`lib/seo.ts`, rendered with `components/shared/JsonLd`), and add every new public page to `app/sitemap.ts`. Tell the owner what changed.
- Guarantee: the only guarantee is the lifetime guarantee on entry points we seal, in the owner's exact wording (`GUARANTEE` in `lib/config/site.ts`). Never paraphrase it. No separate guarantee page, no other guarantees or warranties, no prices.
- Repairs: name only the owner-confirmed repairs in `lib/data/repairs.ts`, which are matched to the home's existing materials and colours. Ask the owner before naming any other repair type.
- No whole-home prevention ("block and lock") on public pages. Repair talk stays limited to the spot the animal used and the damage it caused. An unlisted page for it may come later (see PLACEHOLDERS.md).
- Insurance is real (owner-confirmed, Oct 2026): $5 million liability insurance, in the company's name. Say it only in the exact wording in `INSURANCE` in `lib/config/site.ts` ("$5 million liability insurance"; FAQ answer "Yes. Good Neighbors Wildlife carries $5 million in liability insurance."). Still banned: "fully insured", "bonded", "background-checked", "vetted technicians", "licensed", reviews, ratings, years in business, awards, certifications.
- No other made-up trust signals: no reviews, ratings or certification claims until the owner confirms they are real (see PLACEHOLDERS.md).
- Birds (owner, Oct 12 2026): pigeon jobs on condo and apartment balconies are taken, and the ads may send that traffic to /wildlife/birds. The usual job is removing the birds and the nest; screening a whole balcony is a separate, larger job; building management may need to be involved, depending on the situation. Say it that way, with no prices and no method named for open roofs or ledges until the owner confirms one. Eggs and young are checked for first; protected birds and their nests are left where they are until the young have flown; no relocation promises.
- Phone links, the contact form and Google tracking change only on an explicit request. Tracking: GA4 G-MLBBT02NEC and Google Ads AW-18430229184 share one gtag load. Never use Wasp Problem's G-2NETZXNWVG here.
- Sync with the Google Ads chat (owner's decision, Oct 11 2026: the same way as Wasp Problem): the ads chat cannot see this repo, and there is no shared doc. The owner carries the "For the ads chat" and "For the website Claude Code chat" notes by hand (relay rule below). Each chat keeps its own record: this chat in this file and in `docs/handoffs/`; the ads chat in its own `claude/sync-log.md` in the GNWW Project. Anything from the ads side that the site must keep true (landing-page URLs, ad claims) is written into this file, as the Wasp Problem rules are. The shared Claude Doc from Oct 11 is retired.

Add no render-blocking resource and no always-loaded script without measuring first (`scripts/checks/lighthouse.mjs` and `firstscreen.mjs`). Add a path only with the owner's say-so; do not re-time the tag anywhere else, and never touch the init script that defines `gtag` and queues events (the Ads conversions do nothing if `window.gtag` is missing). The tag's real cost cannot be measured from a cloud session.
